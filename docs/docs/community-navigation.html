Community Navigation

What is in a community's sidebar, and who can see it

0

Two Sidebars

CharDB has two navigation sidebars, and which one you get depends on where you are rather than on anything you set.

The global sidebar is the site: your own characters, galleries and media, what you have liked, your feed, and the browse pages that span every community.

The community sidebar replaces it whenever you are inside a community you belong to. Everything in it belongs to that community.

View Global Navigation, at the top of the community sidebar, swaps back without leaving the page you are on. If you open a community you are not a member of, you keep the global sidebar — there is nothing in the community one you could use.

What Every Member Sees

The Community group is not gated on anything. Being a member of the community is the whole requirement.

  • Browse Characters — every character in this community.
  • My Characters — only yours, arranged into your folders and counted against this community alone. See Character Folders.
  • Members — who else is here.
  • Inventory — the items and currency you hold in this community.
  • Trades — the same offers inbox as the global one, narrowed to this community.
  • Item Ledger — where items came from. Deliberately not staff-only: provenance is readable by anybody so it can act as a trust signal in a trade.
  • Currencies and Shop — what this community mints and what it sells.
The two character links used to be staff-only. They sat under “Species & Characters”, which appears only for members who can create or edit a species — so an ordinary member had no link to a community's characters at all, their own included. That is fixed: both now live in the Community group, where everybody can reach them.
An ordinary member's sidebar — no staff groups at all

What Staff See As Well

Roles carry permissions, and each group below appears only for somebody whose role grants what it needs. A member with none of them sees the sidebar above and nothing more — not a greyed-out list, but no group at all.

  • Invite Codes and Settings join the Community group, for invite and admin permissions respectively.
  • Species & Characters — species management, its traits and its variants. For members who can create or edit a species. When you are looking at one species, its own pages appear here too.
  • Administration — the member dashboard, shop and item administration, permissions, and the moderation queues. Each entry is gated separately, so two staff members with different roles see different lists.

Switching Communities

The box at the very top names the community you are in. Open it for every community you belong to, a search box once there are enough of them to need one, and Browse All Communities for the ones you do not.

Picking one takes you to that community's own address. See Community Subdomains.

The switcher, with a link out to the ones you have not joined
4

Or Do Not Use The Sidebar

Find page… below the switcher, or Ctrl+K, opens a search over every page you can reach. Each community you belong to has its own group in it, named after the community, so typing part of a page name reaches it wherever you currently are.

Typing charac finds the global character pages and each community's, told apart by the group heading and the line underneath. See Fuzzy Page Navigation for what else it does, including @ to search members.
