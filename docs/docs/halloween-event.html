Halloween Event

Trick-or-treat in Discord, run from CharDB

0

Overview

Every October, players knock on the door in the community's Discord server with /knock. Each knock has a chance of winning a prize from the community's prize pool. Players get a few knocks a day, and can gift a friend an extra one.

The game itself happens in Discord. CharDB is where staff run it: set it up, decide what can be won, and keep track of who won what.

It is theirs once they have linked Discord and are a member of your community; until then it waits, and becomes theirs as soon as they do both, in either order.

Turning It On

The event is off for every community until a site admin turns it on. Each community's card in Community Management has a Halloween button that switches it between on and off.

The community also needs its Discord server linked in its settings. That's how CharDB knows which server's event belongs to it.

This only controls CharDB's side. While it's off, the community's staff don't see the event page or its card. The bot keeps answering in Discord either way.

Finding the Event Page

Once the event is on, a Halloween Event card appears on the community's Administration page. It leads to the event page.

Two permissions open the page, and each unlocks its own part:

Permission What it lets you do
Manage items Set the event up, change its settings, and fill the prize pool
Grant items Mark the prizes staff hand over as delivered

Either one lets you see the page: the totals, the prize pool and every win.

The card on the community's Administration page
3

Setting the Event Up

Until the event is set up, the page shows only its settings form. Someone who can only grant items is told it isn't set up yet.

  • Starts and Ends: when knocking opens and closes, in your own time zone. Leave Starts blank to keep it closed, or Ends blank to run until you stop it.
  • Knocks per day: how many knocks each player gets between resets.
  • Reset hour: when everyone's knocks and gifties come back each day, in US Central time.
  • Win rate: the chance that any one knock wins a prize.
  • Win channel ID: optional. A Discord channel where every win is announced, besides the reply to the player. It must be a channel in your community's own server that the bot can see.

Players can knock once the start date has passed and there are prizes in the pool.

The first visit: settings come first
4

The Event Page

Once it's set up, the page has five parts:

  1. Totals: knocks, wins, stock, players, gifties, and prizes still waiting for staff to hand over.
  2. Simulators and a forecast of when the prizes run out, covered in Event Stats and Forecast.
  3. Settings, with Edit settings to change them.
  4. Prize pool: each prize with its picture, how many are left of how many, its weight, and its type.
  5. Wins: every winning knock, newest first, with the winner's Discord account, who gifted the knock if anyone did, their DeviantArt name, and where the prize is.

5

Adding Prizes

Add prize opens the prize form. First pick what kind of prize it is:

Prize type What the winner gets
Item One of the community's items, in their inventory
Character A character nobody owns yet, as their own
Other / off-site Anything else, such as a DeviantArt adopt. Staff hand it over and mark it delivered.

For an item or a character, type at least two letters of its name and pick it from the list. Picking one fills in the prize's Name and Short name for you, unless you've already typed your own.

  • Short name: how the prize is known behind the scenes. 2–25 lowercase letters and hyphens, starting with a letter, and it can't be changed later.
  • Stock: how many can be won. Fungible prizes such as edit kits can have hundreds. A character is one of a kind, so a character prize has no stock to set: it can be won once.
  • Weight: how likely this prize is to be the one won, compared with the others, from 1 to 1000. A prize with more left is proportionally more likely.
Searching the community's items

The character list leaves out characters that are already owned, already promised to someone, or already a prize.

Pictures come from CharDB. An item or character prize shows the item's or character's own picture, once a moderator has approved it. Only an Other / off-site prize takes a picture link.
A character picked: its names are filled in, and there is no stock to set
6

Changing the Prize Pool

  • Edit changes a prize's name, weight, picture link (for an Other / off-site prize) and how many are left. The total moves with it, so the number already won stays right. A new stock is saved only if you changed it, and replaces whatever is left at that moment.
  • A character prize's stock can't be edited. Once its character is won, it stays out of the pool.
  • Remove takes a prize out of the pool for good, after asking. A prize somebody has already won can't be removed, because the win still points at it. Set its stock to 0 instead.
7

Delivering Prizes

Each win's status says where its prize is:

Status Meaning
On chardb: @name An item or character, already with the winner on CharDB
Waiting for them to link Discord An item or character, waiting for the winner to link their Discord account
Waiting for them to join the community An item or character, waiting for the winner, who has linked Discord, to join your community
Staff to deliver An Other / off-site prize nobody has handed over yet
Delivered by @name An Other / off-site prize, with who handed it over and when

Needs delivery narrows the list to the prizes still to hand over. Once you've handed one over, click Mark delivered. If that was a mistake, Undo asks first, then clears who delivered it and when.

In Discord

Players use these commands in the community's server:

Command What it does
/knock Knock for a chance at a prize
/gifty Send a friend one extra knock, once per reset. Also on a member's right-click menu.
/prize list A link to the prize list on CharDB
/info, /help The event's dates and rules, and how to take part
/deviantart Save your DeviantArt name, for prizes delivered there

Server administrators also have /adminknock, to knock on behalf of a player who can't, and /testwin, to check the bot can post in the win channel.

Settings and prizes live on CharDB now. The old /settings and /prize admin commands still appear in Discord for the moment, but only reply with a link to the event page.

When many players knock at the same moment, a knock that can't be answered in time says “Lots of knocking right now. Try again in a moment.” Nothing is used up, so the player can simply knock again.
