Achievements

Badges you earn in your communities, shown on your profile.

0

Overview

Communities hand out achievements for taking part in what they run. Each one belongs to the community it was earned in, and stays on your profile for good.

The first is Trick-or-Treater 2026, for members who sign in during the Halloween event.

An achievement on a profile
1

Earning Trick-or-Treater 2026

Sign in to CharDB while your community's Halloween event is on. It's yours the moment you're in.

  • You need to be a member of the community running the event.
  • Already signed in? Coming back to CharDB on any day of the event counts too.
  • You earn it once per community, dated from the first time.
Signing in during the event
Played before achievements arrived? If you used the candy swap or a candy board during the event, you have it already, dated from when you first did.
2

Seeing Achievements

Open any profile, yours from My Profile in the sidebar or someone else's from their name. Achievements sits under the stats, newest first. Each card shows the community it was earned in and when.

Anyone can see them, signed in or not.
