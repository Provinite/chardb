Overview

Your name appears all over CharDB — on your profile, beside every comment you leave, on the cards that list your characters and your art. Next to it there has always been a circle with your initials in it, and no way to put anything else there.

You can now set a picture. Upload one, choose which square of it to use, and it shows straight away.

Avatars are not moderated, and are not posts. A picture you upload here becomes your avatar the moment it is sent. It never appears in your media, on your profile's media strip, or in anyone’s feed, and it does not go to a moderation queue.
1

Finding It

The avatar sits at the top of Edit Profile, above your display name and bio. It saves the moment you upload — there is no need to press Save Changes further down the page.

You can reach this page from a community as well as from the main site. It is the same page and the same single profile either way: what you change here shows everywhere, not just in the community you happened to be in. The page says so when you arrive from one.

The Avatar section, with an avatar already set
2

Uploading and Framing One

Upload a picture asks for a file and then offers a square frame over it. Drag the picture to reposition it, or use the zoom slider to tighten in.

This matters more than it sounds. Character art is usually full-body, and the middle of a full-body drawing is a torso. Framing on the face is the difference between an avatar that is recognisable at 32 pixels and one that is a coloured smudge.

Framing on the face rather than accepting the middle
3

Changing or Removing It

Adjust framing reopens the square frame over the whole picture, starting from where you left it, so you can move in on a different part without uploading again.

To use a different picture, upload it. The new one replaces the old one, and the old one is deleted — an avatar you have moved on from is not kept anywhere.

To use art you have already posted, upload the file again. An avatar is always its own copy with its own framing, so adjusting your avatar never moves the thumbnail of a post.

Remove deletes the picture and puts your initials back.

4

Where It Shows Up

Everywhere your name appears: your profile heading, the corner of every page while you are signed in, your comments, the member lists of the communities you belong to, and the cards that credit you as an owner or an artist.
