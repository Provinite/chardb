Watching Characters

Hear when a character you want goes up for trade or sale

0

Overview

Watch another member's character and the bell tells you whenever its owner changes what it is open to: for sale, for sale for community currency, open to character trades, open to art trades, open to offers, or free to a good home. A new asking price on a character for sale counts too.

👁️

Watch

One button on the character page.

🔔

Notified

One notification per save, naming what changed.

🔒

Private stays private

A character made private notifies nobody.

1

Watching a character

Watch sits under the like button on any character you can see but don't own. Press it again, now reading Watching, to stop.

A watched character. The button reads Watching.
2

The notification

The notification names who made the change and what moved, and opens the character. One save that changes several things is one notification:

  • Owner says Marrowfen is now for sale for $45.00 and open to offers
  • Owner says Marrowfen is no longer free to a good home
  • Owner changed Marrowfen's price to $30.00

Renames, new images and other edits leave watchers alone. So does your own edit to a character you watch.

For sale and open to offers, in one save.
3

Private characters

You can only watch a character you can see. If its owner later makes it private, its changes stop reaching you, including the save that made it private. Your watch stays, and resumes if the character is made visible again.

4

Turning it off

Watched characters has its own switch under Edit Profile → Notifications. It turns off every watch at once, without forgetting which characters you watch. These notifications are in-app only. See Notification Preferences.

The Watched characters row, on by default.
