External Members

Finding people who hold things here without an account

0

Overview

Items and characters can be granted to a Discord or DeviantArt account before its owner has an account here. Event prizes handed out by the Discord bot work this way. Until that person links the account and joins, what they won waits on the account.

External Members, under Administration, is where staff find those people: by name, by ID, or by browsing. Opening one shows everything waiting on them, and staff can remove items they have already spent elsewhere.

Who can use it: staff whose role can see pending owners (the create orphaned characters permission). Removing items also needs grant items.
1

Looking up Discord names

Grants record the Discord username and display name of whoever they go to. Older grants recorded only the numeric Discord ID, which nobody can search for by name.

A site admin fixes that once from Site Administration → Discord Names. Look up Discord names asks Discord about every account something waits on and records both names. Run it again later to pick up names people have changed.

When it finishes it reports how many accounts were named, how many Discord no longer knows, and how many it couldn't reach Discord for. Those last ones are worth a second run.

The Discord Names page, before a run.
2

Finding someone

Start typing. Search matches the display name or username anywhere in the name, or the account ID exactly — paste an ID straight from Discord. Any account narrows it to Discord or DeviantArt.

Each result is one person, with how many items and characters wait on them.

Searching for moth.
3

Browsing everyone

Browse all lists everyone, newest grant first, 25 to a page. Typing a search narrows the same list.

Someone listed only by a number, like Discord 214000000000010003, has no names recorded yet. The name lookup in step 1 fills them in.

Everyone, newest first. The middle row predates name lookups.
4

Their pending inventory

Opening someone shows what waits on them: items grouped by type, and characters. Their name on the item ledger links here too.

Everything here moves to them automatically once they link that Discord or DeviantArt account and join the community.

Six items in three types, and a character.
5

Removing what they've spent

Remove… takes some or all of one item type. Say how many, and give a reason — members see it on the ledger. A staff note is optional and stays with staff. The oldest go first.

Removing both Hollowtide Candies.

Each removal is one entry on the item ledger, naming who it was taken from.

The ledger, searched for moth.
