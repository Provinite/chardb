Notification Preferences

Choose what reaches you, and how

Overview

Every kind of notification has two switches: one for the bell and feed, and one for email. They are independent. Turning off the mail about approved images does not stop the app telling you an image was approved, and it does not touch anything else.

Your settings live on your profile, under Edit Profile → Notifications.

In app

The bell, the dropdown, and the notifications page.

✉️

Email

A message to your address, with a way out in the footer.

🔒

Always sent

Password resets and password-change confirmations.

1

The matrix

One row per kind, one column per channel. A change saves as you make it — there is no save button and nothing to submit.

Edit Profile → Notifications, at the defaults.
An em dash instead of a switch means that kind is not emailed at all yet, so there is nothing to turn off. Those rows grow a switch when the email behind them is built, rather than offering one now that would do nothing.
2

What you get by default

Defaults are chosen per kind rather than set globally, and the question they answer is whether the message asks anything of you.

Kind In app Email Why
Image approved On Off The expected outcome. You already see the result in the app, and there is nothing to do about it.
Image not approved On On Actionable: it carries a reason, and often a moderator's own words about what to fix.
Everything else On — Follows, comments, items, currency and trades reach the bell. None of them email yet.
Changing a default changes it for you only if you have never touched that switch. Once you set one, it is yours and a later change of default leaves it alone.
3

Getting here from an email

Every optional email ends with a line pointing back at these settings:

Don't want these? Choose which emails you get from your profile settings.

The link opens the settings above, where you can switch off the one kind that prompted the mail — or any other. Signing in is part of it, because these are your account's settings and changing them is not something a link out of an inbox should be able to do on its own.

There is no one-click unsubscribe, and deliberately so. This is mail about your own uploads rather than marketing, so no opt-out mechanism is required of it, and a one-click link would mean a signed token and an endpoint that acts on it with nobody signed in. A link to the settings page does the same job.
4

What you cannot turn off

Two emails have no switch and no settings link:

  • Password reset — the message is the mechanism. Suppressing it would lock you out.
  • Password changed — how you find out that somebody else changed it.

These are not listed in the matrix with the switch greyed out; they are named under Always sent instead, so the absence reads as a decision rather than an oversight.

This is structural, not a flag. Security mail is sent by a code path that has no access to your preferences at all, so no setting and no bug in one can stop a password reset arriving.
