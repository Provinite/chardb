Halloween Candy Board

Nobody home? Take a candy. Stick it on your board.

Overview

During the community's Halloween event, you knock on doors in Discord with /knock. When nobody answers, you get a candy instead.

Candy boards are where those candies go. Stick them on, pile them up, pick a background, set the light, name your board, then share or download it.

Your Boards

While the event runs, Candy Boards in the community sidebar lists your boards, each with a preview.

  • New board starts an empty one. Up to ten each.
  • Every board draws on your whole collection. Using a candy on one board doesn't take it from another.
  • Deleting a board loses only the board. Candies stay yours.

Candies come from Discord, so boards need Discord linked to your CharDB account.

Boards are unlisted. Each has a random address, so only people you send it to can find it.

Sticking Candies On

Under the board, Your candies holds whatever isn't stuck on yet, one tile per kind with a count. Tap a tile to stick that candy in the middle, or drag it to where you want it. Take a candy off and it goes back in the tray.

One tile per kind
3

Arranging

Click a sticker to pick it up. Drag it to move it, drag a corner to resize it, and drag the handle above it to turn it (Shift snaps to 15°).

Stickers pile up. Clicking a see-through edge reaches what's beneath, and clicking a picked-up sticker again picks the one under it. Click off the board to let go.

The toolbar under the board has rotate and scale sliders, buttons to move a sticker up or down the pile, and one to take it off.

Picked up, with handles and toolbar
Keys On the picked-up sticker
← ↑ → ↓ Nudge; with Shift, further
[ ] Turn 15°
- = Shrink, grow
Delete Backspace Back to the tray
Changes save as you go. If one can't get through, a Retry button appears and nothing is lost.

Clear board takes everything off at once, after asking.

Background

New boards start on graph paper. Background offers pictures and repeating tiles, previewed under your board's light.

If a picture is too loud for your candies, turn it down with Saturation and Dim, or wash it in one colour with Tint. Nothing changes until you press Use background.

Previewing a picture
5

Light

Every sticker casts a shadow. Lighting opens a panel, and the board follows as you change it.

  • Drag the sun around the circle to move the light. Shadows fall away from it.
  • Height sets shadow length; Intensity, how dark they are.
  • Colour washes the whole board in coloured light.
  • Spotlight darkens all but a pool of light, and Cone sets its width. The pool lands opposite the sun, outlined on the circle.
Spotlight on
6

Name and Nameplate

Name builds a name from Halloween words: a first word, an optional joiner, a last word. Stuck? Shuffle. The name becomes your board's title.

A named board gets a nameplate at the front of its tray: your name on a ribbon, stuck on and moved like any candy. Long names stretch the ribbon. Rename the board and the ribbon follows. Remove the name and it comes off, returning to the same spot if you name the board again.

Word by word
7

Sharing and Downloading

Copy link copies your board's address. Anyone with it sees the board as you left it, signed in or not, but can't change anything.

Download saves your board as a 2400 × 1800 PNG, light and all. Visitors can download it too.
