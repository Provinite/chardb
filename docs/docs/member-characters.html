A Member's Characters and Galleries

Everything one person owns, on one page, filtered to what you may see

0

Overview

A profile shows a handful of recent characters and a handful of recent galleries. To see the rest, open View All beside either heading, or click the Characters or Galleries tile in the stats row. Both go to a page listing everything that member owns.

This used to go somewhere else. "View All" pointed at the site-wide browse page with a filter that was silently discarded, so it showed every character on CharDB rather than that member's. The count beside the link was right, which made the mismatch look like a data problem rather than a broken link.
Two ways in: the stats tiles at the top, and "View All" beside the section heading
1

The Listing

The page is titled with whose characters they are, and says how many it is showing out of how many there are. Long lists load a page at a time — use Load More at the bottom to fetch the next batch.

Cards do not repeat the owner's name, because every card on the page has the same owner. Back to profile returns you where you came from.

The destination: only that member's characters, counted and titled
2

What You Can See

The list is filtered by who is asking, not just by who owns it. What appears depends on your relationship to the member whose page you are on:

  • Their own page, signed in as them: everything — public, unlisted and private.
  • Someone else's page: their public characters and galleries only.
  • Signed out: the same public-only view. The page is open to visitors; it does not ask you to log in.
Unlisted is not listed. An unlisted character stays reachable by its link — that is what unlisted means — but it does not appear in anyone else's listing of your characters, and it is not counted in the number on your profile. Only private hides it from the link as well.

The filtering happens on the server. A character you are not allowed to see is never sent to your browser at all, rather than sent and then hidden.
