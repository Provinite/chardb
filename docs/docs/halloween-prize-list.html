Overview

While the Halloween event runs, /halloween/prizes on the community's site lists every prize in the pool beside the latest wins. Anyone can open it, signed in or not.

Members reach it from Prize List in the sidebar's Halloween section. On Discord, /prize list replies with a link to it.

The prize list, signed out
1

Prizes

Each card shows the prize's picture, its name and how many are left of how many there were. Prizes still in stock come first, by name; those all won follow, greyed out, with an All won badge.

A prize that is a CharDB item or character links to it with View on chardb. Draw odds aren't shown.

2

Recent Wins

The twenty latest wins, newest first: the prize, who won it and how long ago. The list refreshes every minute.

Who won depends on who's looking:

  • A winner whose Discord is linked to CharDB shows as their CharDB name, linked to their profile, for everyone.
  • Anyone else shows as A Discord user, to visitors and to members.
  • Staff who can grant or manage items get that winner linked to their Discord profile, under their Discord handle when the prize is one staff hand over.
