Halloween Candy Swap

Got three candy corns and want a banana? Trade for it.

0

Overview

The candies your /knocks leave you during the Halloween event are yours to trade. While the event runs, Candy Swap in the community sidebar opens the swap page.

You put up one to three candies for one kind you want, or for anything at all. Other players see your offer without your name on it, and take it if they have what you asked for. Once a trade goes through, both of you see whom it was with.

Candies come from Discord, so you need Discord linked and to be a member of the community before any are yours.

Taking an Offer

Offers you can take shows a random handful of other players' offers, only ones you hold a spare candy for. Each card shows what's on offer, then what it wants. Show others deals a new handful.

Trade your … trades straight away: you give one candy of the wanted kind, one that isn't on a board if you have one, and get everything on the card.

An asterisk in place of the wanted candy means the offer takes any kind. Pick a candy to trade opens your spare candies; tap the one to give and the trade goes through. Kinds the offer gives are greyed out.

Making an Offer

Under Make an offer, tap candies in Your candies to put them in the three Giving places, and tap one there to take it back. Then tap the Asking for slot, pick a candy, and press Place offer.

The picker shows every candy, with a count on the ones you have. The asterisk at the start asks for anything: handy when you have too many of one kind and don't mind what comes back. Picking your current choice again clears it.

Picking what to ask for
  • One to three candies, for exactly one.
  • You can't ask for a kind you're giving, or give a kind you're asking for.
  • Up to ten open offers at once. They don't expire.
More candies, better odds. Nobody can ask for more than one candy, but you can offer up to three to make yours more tempting.
Two candy corns for anything

Open Offers

Candies you've offered wait in the offer. They leave your tray and your candy boards, and can't go in a second offer. Cancel gives them back.

An offer waiting to be taken
Mirror offers trade themselves. If you offer one candy for one, and someone already offers exactly what you want for exactly what you're giving, the trade happens as you place it. An offer for anything counts: one candy for anything matches an offer asking for that candy, and the other way round. Two offers for anything never match each other. With several matches, one is picked at random. Offers of two or three candies only trade when someone takes them.
4

Your Trades

Your trades lists every swap you've made, newest first: whom it was with, when, what you gave and what you got.

Whom you traded with

When someone takes one of your offers, or your offer is taken by a matching one, you get a notification naming them. It opens the swap page.

Hearing that an offer was taken

Turn these off under Candy swaps in your notification settings.
